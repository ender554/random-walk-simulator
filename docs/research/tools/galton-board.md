# Galton Board: feature research (2026-10-28)

**How to be the best:** the only Galton board that ties one ball's path to Pascal's triangle path counts, then to the binomial bars and the normal curve, all in one view. It should be fast, work on phones and projectors, and be shareable by link, so a teacher can go from one coin flip to a bell curve without switching tools.

Competitors:
- PhET Plinko: drop 1, 10 or 100 balls; statistics panel; count/fraction toggle; pegs that tilt with p; sound.
- WaveSpeed: bursts, a normal overlay, chi-squared, CSV and video export.
- iPad app: compare boards side by side.
- Nobody overlays Pascal's triangle live, and nobody has a share link, a projector mode or a speed slider.

Evidence:
- PhET staff: "Sliders are very tiny".
- A request for 1-row boards, to show that each peg is one coin flip.
- App reviews ask for "a slightly faster speed", a repeat button, and why runs differ.
- itch.io sim: no mobile support.

Must:
- Drop +1, +10 and +100 balls, and continuous play/pause.
- A speed slider.
- The last ball's path, with an L/R readout.
- A Pascal's triangle overlay.
- Large touch controls and a projector mode.

Should:
- A count / fraction / probability toggle.
- A normal overlay, plus mean and ±1σ markers.
- A share link.
- CSV and high-res PNG export.
- Rows down to 1.

Could:
- Two boards side by side.
- Sound.
- A chi-squared test.
